# Writing

The drafting companion and the handoff entry point. Section dispositions, locked verbatim text and dictations, vocabulary rulings, style rules, word budgets, and the TODO-before-drafting ledger. A fresh drafting session starts from this file and the final exhibits, never from chat history.

Boundary. Writing owns the locked text and its placement. The decision behind the text, and its rationale, lives in arguments.
