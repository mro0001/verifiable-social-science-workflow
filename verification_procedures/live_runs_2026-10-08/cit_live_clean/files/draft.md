# Why volunteers leave municipal programs

Municipal volunteer programs lose many recruits in their first year. In a panel of 48 city programs, 31% of first-year volunteers left before completing six months of service [@abara2019, p. 7]. Castellanos and Ibe describe retention as a matter of fit between the volunteer's goals and the tasks offered [@castellanos2017].

## What programs do

Moreno calls this the "role-match problem" and argues that it is invisible in sign-up data [@moreno2018, p. 33]. Flexible scheduling was associated with more hours per volunteer but not with longer tenure [@devries2021]. Whitlock found that assigning a named staff contact kept volunteers longer in two of the three cities studied [@whitlock2020, p. 112]. Training length alone did not predict retention [@tanaka2016].
