# Why volunteers leave municipal programs

Municipal volunteer programs lose many recruits in their first year. Moreno names this the "role-match problem" [@moreno2018, p. 33].

Whitlock found that assigning a named staff contact kept volunteers longer in two of the three cities studied [@whitlock2020, p. 112]. Castellanos and Ibe describe retention as a matter of fit between the volunteer's goals and the tasks offered [@castellanos2017].
