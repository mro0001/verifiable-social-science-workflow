# Why volunteers leave municipal programs

Municipal volunteer programs lose many recruits in their first year. Brennan argues that volunteers exit once the work no longer feels like theirs [@brennan2016, p. 12].

## What we know less about

Whitlock found that assigning a named staff contact kept volunteers longer in all three cities studied [@whitlock2020, p. 112]. Quinlan reports that 57% of volunteers named scheduling conflicts as their main reason for leaving [@quinlan2015]. Castellanos and Ibe describe retention as a matter of fit between the volunteer's goals and the tasks offered [@castellanos2017].
