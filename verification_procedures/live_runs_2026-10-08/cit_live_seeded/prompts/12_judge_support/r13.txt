Know this before acting:
- verify_quote -> judge_support: Judge the claim as worded now against the located passage and its context. supports = the passage says what the sentence says; partly = it says less, or with conditions the sentence drops; does_not_support = it says something else or the opposite.

    avoid: Real citations do not mean correct use (Linardon 2026, 19.8% claim-citation errors with 100% real citations).
    avoid: Anachronism, citing a standard dated after the event the sentence describes (ICMJE 2013 case).
    avoid: A number that appears in the source but in a different table or population than the claim says (Kapoor case).
    avoid: Abstract-only checks overstated as full verification.
- judge_support -> collect_verdicts: A row is a problem when any blocking finding touches it or its key, when the reference metadata is wrong, or when the verdict is not supports.
    avoid: Letting a supports verdict hide a mechanical failure on the same row.
