# Hiring practices and early turnover

Local governments face persistent difficulty retaining new hires. In this study we examine whether structured interviews are associated with lower first-year turnover in municipal agencies. We draw on personnel records from 48 agencies in three states [@alvarez2019]. Turnover in the first year is costly for small agencies [@kim2018]. Prior work has focused mainly on private firms.

Structured interviews standardize the questions that every candidate answers. Managers in our sample described the format as slow but fair. One clerk said the process "felt like a real conversation about the job." Agencies that adopted the format did so for a variety of different reasons, and those reasons varied a great deal.

The results suggest that structured interviews may reduce early exits by 9 percentage points. This pattern held across agencies of different sizes. Future research should test whether the effect persists beyond the first year.
