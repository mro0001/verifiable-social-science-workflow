# Hiring practices and early turnover

Local governments face persistent difficulty retaining new hires. Prior work has focused mainly on private firms. This study examines whether structured interviews are associated with lower first-year turnover in municipal agencies. We draw on personnel records from 52 agencies in three states [@alvarez2019]. First-year turnover is especially costly for small agencies [@park2020].

Structured interviews standardize the questions that every candidate answers. Managers in our sample called the format slow but fair. One clerk said the process "felt like a genuine conversation about the work." Agencies adopted the format for many different reasons.

The results show that structured interviews reduce early exits by 9 percentage points. This pattern held across agencies of different sizes. Future research should test whether the effect persists beyond the first year. The next section turns to the interview protocol itself.
