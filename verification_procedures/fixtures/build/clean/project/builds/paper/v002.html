1 Introduction

Volunteer fire districts rely on meetings to carry lessons from one call to the next (Ardent 2019; Boyle 2021). We ask whether meeting more often helps teams learn.

2 Data

We surveyed 48 districts and 1,214 members in the spring wave.

3 Results

Districts that met monthly reported higher learning scores than districts that met quarterly (standardized difference 0.31, 95% interval [0.12, 0.50]).

4 Limitations

The survey is cross-sectional, so cadence may follow learning rather than cause it.
