# Introduction

Volunteer fire districts rely on meetings to carry lessons from one call to the next [@ardent2019; @boyle2021]. We ask whether meeting more often helps teams learn.

# Data

We surveyed 46 districts and 1,190 members in the spring wave.

# Results

Districts that met monthly reported higher learning scores than districts that met quarterly (standardized difference 0.31, 95% interval {{num:ci_monthly}}).

# Limitations

The survey is cross-sectional, so cadence may follow learning rather than cause it.
