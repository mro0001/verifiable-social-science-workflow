Ownership and exit in municipal volunteer programs
Nkem Abara and Teresa Hollis (SYNTHETIC)

[p. 5]
We followed every volunteer who joined one of the programs in our panel during a single recruitment year and recorded the date of their last logged shift.

[p. 7]
Across the 48 programs in our panel, 31% of first-year volunteers exited before completing six months of service. Exit was concentrated in the first eight weeks.
