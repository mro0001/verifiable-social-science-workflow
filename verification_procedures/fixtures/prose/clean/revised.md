# Hiring practices and early turnover

Local governments face persistent difficulty retaining new hires. Prior work has focused mainly on private firms. This study examines whether structured interviews are associated with lower first-year turnover in municipal agencies. We draw on personnel records from 48 agencies in three states [@alvarez2019].

Structured interviews ask every candidate the same questions in the same order. Managers in our sample described the format as slow but fair. One clerk said the process "felt like a real conversation about the job."

The results suggest that structured interviews may reduce early exits by 9 percentage points. This pattern held across agencies of different sizes. Future research should test whether the effect persists beyond the first year. The next section turns to the interview protocol itself.
