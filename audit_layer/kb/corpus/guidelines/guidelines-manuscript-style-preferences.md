# Manuscript style preferences

These are the author's standing prose rules for manuscripts and drafts. Any session that drafts or edits prose applies them without being asked, in every deliverable.

1. Do not use em-dashes, and do not use colons inside prose sentences. Both read as AI tells.
2. State the claim first, then cite in parenthetical author-year form. Narrative citation is never the default.
3. Match the author's voice as it already stands in the manuscript rather than imposing a house style.
4. Never fabricate or embellish a quantitative claim. A number that cannot be traced to a source or a computation does not enter the prose.
5. Prefer plain declarative sentences over hedged or ornamental phrasing.
